1. Purpose

This policy sets how Harlow Fabrication Ltd buys machines, tools and other equipment. It explains who may approve a purchase, how many quotes are needed and what records must be kept.

The policy applies to every site and every budget. Software licences follow the IT Purchasing Standard and are not covered here.

2. Roles

The requester describes the need and the budget line. The buyer collects quotes and prepares the purchase order. The approver checks that the purchase is needed, priced fairly and within budget.

No person may approve a purchase that they requested. When the usual approver is absent, the approver one level higher signs instead.

3. Approval limits

The total value of the purchase order, excluding tax, decides who must approve it.

Table 1 - Approval limits

Order value (GBP)ApproverQuotes needed
Up to 5,000Line managerOne written quote
5,001 to 40,000Head of FinanceTwo written quotes
Above 40,000Managing DirectorThree written quotes

Splitting one purchase into several smaller orders to stay under a limit is not allowed and is treated as a breach of this policy.

4. Quotes and supplier choice

Quotes must be written and must describe the same equipment, delivery date and warranty, so that they can be compared fairly.

The buyer chooses the offer with the best value over the life of the equipment, not only the lowest price. Running costs, spare parts and service support are part of that value.

When fewer quotes than required are available, the buyer writes a short note explaining why, and the approver signs the note together with the order.

5. Emergency purchases

When a machine breaks down and production stops, the site manager may order the repair or replacement part without quotes.

The site manager informs the Head of Finance within two working days and the normal approval is completed afterwards.

Planned replacements are never treated as emergency purchases, even when the old machine is close to failing.

6. New suppliers

Before a new supplier receives its first order, Finance checks the supplier's bank details, insurance certificate and company registration.

Finance also screens every new supplier against the national sanctions list.

  • Suppliers rated High risk are reviewed every year.
  • Suppliers rated Medium risk are reviewed every two years.
  • Suppliers rated Low risk are reviewed when their contract is renewed.

7. Records

The buyer keeps the quotes, the purchase order, the approval and the delivery note for eight years after the order is closed.

Records are kept in the finance system, not in personal email folders.

8. Breaches

Purchases made without following this policy are reported to the Head of Finance. Repeated breaches are reported to the Managing Director and may lead to disciplinary action.

Internal Audit checks a sample of purchase orders against this policy at least once a year.
