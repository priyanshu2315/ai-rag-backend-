Beyond the reference

  • Hybrid search: pgvector plus Postgres full-text, fused and re-ranked with Cohere in rerankChunks().
  • Corrective loop: gradeDocumentsNode() grades all chunks together, and rewriteQueryNode() retries with a new search.
  • Untrusted-text wrappers: retrieved text and questions sit inside random nonce tags in generateNode().
  • Live progress: publishProgress() and streamProgress() for uploads, emit() for every answer step.
