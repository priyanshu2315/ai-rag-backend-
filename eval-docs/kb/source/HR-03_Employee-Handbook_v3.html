1. Welcome

Welcome to Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd. Calderwick designs, builds and tests industrial pumps for water utilities, mining and process industries. We employ around 610 people at our Head Office and three plants: Plant P1 at Hollins Cross assembles pumps, Plant P2 at Ardmoor tests and calibrates them on hydraulic test rigs, and Plant P3 at Fenwick Bay produces castings.

This handbook explains how we work together: hours, pay, leave, conduct, safety, information security, and what to do when something goes wrong. It applies to every employee of Calderwick. Contractors and agency workers follow the sections on conduct, safety and information security while they work on our sites.

3. Working hours

Office-based employees work 37.5 hours a week, Monday to Friday. Flexitime applies at the Head Office: you may start between 07:30 and 10:00 and finish between 15:00 and 19:00, provided you are present during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00. Lunch breaks of at least 30 minutes are unpaid.

Plant employees work the shift patterns of their plant. Shift times are set by the Plant Manager and may change with production needs after at least two weeks' notice.

Table 1 - Plant shift patterns

Overtime must be approved in advance by your line manager. Hourly paid employees receive 1.5 times the hourly rate for overtime on weekdays and Saturdays, and 2 times the hourly rate on Sundays and public holidays. Salaried employees do not receive overtime pay but may agree time off in lieu with their line manager.

4. Starting and leaving

4.1 Probation

New employees serve a probation period of six months. Managers and heads of department serve nine months. During probation the notice period is one week on either side. Your line manager reviews your progress at three months and at the end of probation; the People team confirms the outcome in writing.

4.2 Notice periods

After probation, the notice periods below apply unless your contract states a longer period.

4.3 Leaving Calderwick

When you leave, return your badge, laptop, phone, tools and personal protective equipment to your line manager on your last working day. The HR Business Partner offers every leaver an exit interview. IT disables your accounts at the end of your last working day; your line manager must raise the leaver request at least five working days before. Final pay, including any unused holiday, is paid on the next normal pay date.

5. Pay and benefits

Salaries are paid monthly on the 25th of each month, or the working day before if the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday. Payslips are available in the HR portal. Questions about pay go to the Payroll Manager.

Calderwick pays an employer pension contribution of 6% of basic salary when the employee contributes at least 4%. All employees who have passed probation are covered by the company health plan. Life cover of four times basic salary applies from the first day of employment.

6. Absence and sickness

If you cannot come to work, tell your line manager by phone by 09:30 on the first day of absence, or at least one hour before your shift starts if you work shifts. A message through a colleague is not enough.

For absences of up to seven calendar days you complete a self-certification form in the HR portal. For longer absences you need a medical certificate. After any absence of more than three working days, your line manager holds a short return-to-work meeting with you on your first day back.

Company sick pay is full pay for up to 20 working days in any rolling 12 months, after which statutory sick pay applies.

7. Holidays and leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave plus public holidays. The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March, the same as our fiscal year. The Leave Policy and Holiday Calendar (HR-LV-2025) contains the full rules, including carry-over, long-service days, plant shutdowns and the public holidays for the year.

8. Conduct and conflicts of interest

We expect everyone at Calderwick to act honestly, treat colleagues and partners with respect and follow the law and our policies. Bullying, harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

A conflict of interest exists when your personal interests, or those of a close family member, could influence a decision you make for Calderwick. You must declare any such interest in writing to your line manager and to the General Counsel before you take part in the decision. Examples are a family member working for a supplier, or a shareholding in a competitor.

8.1 Gifts and hospitality

You may accept gifts and hospitality worth up to USD 50 per occasion from a business partner. Anything above USD 50 must be declared in the gifts register kept by the General Counsel within five working days, whether you accept it or not. You must never accept any gift or hospitality from a supplier taking part in a tender in which you are involved, whatever its value.

9. Health and safety

Safety comes first at every Calderwick site. Follow the safety rules and signs of the area you are in and wear the personal protective equipment (PPE) shown at the entrance of each zone. Safety boots and high-visibility clothing are required on all plant floors; hearing protection is required in zones marked as noisy, including the Ardmoor test halls when a rig is running.

Only people holding a current certificate may operate forklifts, overhead cranes or hydraulic test rigs. Forklift and crane certificates are valid for three years and must be renewed before they expire; an operator whose certificate has lapsed may not use the equipment until the renewal is recorded in the training register.

Report every accident, injury and near miss to your supervisor at once and record it in the safety system within 24 hours. The Health and Safety Manager reviews every report and publishes a summary each quarter.

10. IT and information

Calderwick systems and data are for business use. Limited personal use of email and internet is allowed outside working time if it does not break any law or policy. Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access.

Never share your password, plug unknown USB devices into plant equipment or install software yourself. Report suspicious emails to the IT service desk using the report button. The IT Security Policy (IT-SEC-02) contains the full rules, including the rules for plant control systems.

11. Grievances

If you have a concern about your work, colleagues or how you have been treated, raise it informally with your line manager first; most concerns are solved this way.

If the concern is not resolved, or if it is about your line manager, raise a formal grievance in writing to your line manager or to your HR Business Partner within 20 working days of the event. A grievance meeting is held within 10 working days of receiving it, and you may bring a colleague or trade union representative. You receive the outcome in writing. If you disagree with the outcome you may appeal in writing to the Head of People within 5 working days; the appeal decision is final.

12. Disciplinary procedure

Where conduct or performance falls short of what is expected, managers first try to resolve the issue informally. If formal action is needed, the stages below apply. Each stage follows an investigation and a meeting at which you may be accompanied.

Gross misconduct can lead to dismissal without notice. Examples are theft or fraud, violence, serious breach of safety rules, working under the influence of alcohol or drugs, and accepting bribes.

13. Speaking up (whistleblowing)

If you suspect fraud, bribery, a serious safety risk, a breach of law or a cover-up, report it. You can report to your line manager, to the General Counsel, or anonymously through the independent Clearline hotline, which is available 24 hours a day by phone and web. Calderwick does not tolerate any retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith; retaliation is itself a disciplinary matter.

The General Counsel reviews every report, decides who investigates and reports a summary of cases to the Audit Committee each quarter without naming the people who reported.

14. Personal data

Calderwick keeps personal data about employees only for employment, pay, legal and safety purposes, and only for as long as needed. Employment records are kept for six years after an employee leaves. You can ask the Head of People for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

15. Changes to this handbook

The Head of People reviews this handbook every two years, or earlier when the law or a referenced policy changes. Changes are announced on the intranet and take effect on the date stated in the announcement.
