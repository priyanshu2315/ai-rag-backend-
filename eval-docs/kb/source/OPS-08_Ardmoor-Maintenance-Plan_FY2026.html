1. Purpose

This plan sets the preventive maintenance, inspections and critical spares for the hydraulic test rigs at Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd's Ardmoor plant for fiscal year 2026. Preventive and corrective maintenance are carried out by Brennock Valve Services Ltd under contract CON-2024-031.

3. Pressure system inspections

Each rig's pressure system is inspected once a year by the competent person, the Maintenance Lead, Ardmoor, who holds the pressure systems competent person certificate. TR-5 receives its first Ardmoor inspection before customer tests at pressures above 40 bar.
