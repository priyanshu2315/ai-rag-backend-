1. Purpose

This directory maps every management and specialist role at Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd to the person who holds it. Policies, contracts and approval rules name roles, not people; use this directory to find the person behind a role. Each person has a unique employee ID. Where two names look alike, the employee ID identifies the person.

The directory is valid from 1 July 2025 until the next edition is issued. Changes during that period, including acting appointments, are listed in section 3.
