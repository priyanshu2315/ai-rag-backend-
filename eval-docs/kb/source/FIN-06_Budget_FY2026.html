1. Summary

The FY2026 budget of Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd plans revenue of USD 98.5 million (up 6.6% on FY2025), EBITDA of USD 14.2 million and capital expenditure of USD 7.4 million. Amounts are in USD million unless stated otherwise.

6. Main assumptions

  • Exchange rate USD 1.08 per EUR for euro-denominated sales.
  • Steel castings input prices up 4% on FY2025.
  • Average pay increase of 3.5% from 1 April 2025.
  • Ardmoor rig availability of at least 97%, with TR-1 to TR-4 in use and a fifth rig, TR-5, from October 2025.
  • Fenwick Bay closed for furnace relining for three weeks in November 2025.
