1. Summary

Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd closed the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 with revenue of USD 23.6 million, USD 0.4 million below budget, and EBITDA of USD 3.3 million against a budget of USD 3.4 million. Pump orders were in line with plan; the shortfall came from spare parts sales, where two large mining customers delayed orders into the second quarter.

All amounts are in USD million unless stated otherwise. The fiscal year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

4. Operations and people

Hollins Cross assembled 3,640 pumps in the quarter. Ardmoor completed 452 customer tests with average rig availability of 97.6%. Fenwick Bay poured 2,310 tonnes of castings.

Headcount was 618 on 30 June 2025, against a budget of 620. The new IT Service Manager and the Project Manager for the Ardmoor Rig Upgrade join on 1 July 2025.

5. Matters for the next quarter

  • The amended Purchase Approval Authority Matrix (FIN-AAM-02) applies to purchase orders raised from 1 July 2025.
  • Project PRJ-11, the Ardmoor rig control upgrade, was approved by the Board on 19 June 2025 with a budget of USD 480,000; engineering orders are expected in August.
  • The Fenwick Bay furnace relining (PRJ-14) is planned for November 2025 and needs Board approval of its budget in September.
