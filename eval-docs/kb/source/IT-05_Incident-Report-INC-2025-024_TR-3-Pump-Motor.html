1. Summary

On 7 October 2025 the 75 kW hydraulic pump motor of rig TR-3 at the Ardmoor plant of Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd tripped and would not restart, stopping a customer test. The service desk logged a P1 incident. The supplier's on-call engineer arrived within the response time and replaced the motor with the spare from the Ardmoor critical spares store.

2. Timeline (7 October 2025)

3. Measured times

Measured from the service desk log at 13:20: the supplier engineer arrived after 1 hour 30 minutes and the rig was back in service after 7 hours. The incident was logged after 1 October 2025, so the amended service levels of CON-2024-031-A1 apply.

4. Root cause

The motor winding failed from insulation breakdown after about 31,000 running hours, close to the end of its expected life. The motors of TR-1, TR-2 and TR-4 have between 18,000 and 24,000 hours.
