1. Purpose

This directory maps the management and specialist roles at Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd to the people who hold them on 1 January 2025. Policies and contracts name roles; this directory names the person behind each role for the period it covers. Where two names look alike, the employee ID identifies the person.
