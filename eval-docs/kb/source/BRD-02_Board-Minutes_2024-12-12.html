2. Half-year results

The CFO reported revenue for the first half of FY2025 (April to September 2024) of USD 44.7 million, ahead of budget, with test and calibration services growing fastest after the second shift on rig TR-4 started in September.

3. Procurement Policy

The Head of Procurement, attending for this item, presented Version 4 of the Procurement Policy of Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd. The main changes are a formal tender for purchases above USD 100,000, rules for framework call-offs, and supplier risk ratings with annual General Counsel review of High-risk suppliers.

Resolution 2024-11: The Board approved the Procurement Policy, Version 4 (PRC-POL-04), with effect from 1 January 2025.

4. Internal audit

The Audit Committee recommended outsourcing internal audit after a tender in which three firms bid.

Resolution 2024-12: The Board appointed Fairholm Assurance LLP as internal auditor for three years from 1 January 2025 (contract CON-2024-052) and asked the Audit Committee to approve its annual plan.

5. Next meeting

The next meeting will be held on 13 March 2025.
