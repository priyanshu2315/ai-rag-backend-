CALDERWICK PUMP SYSTEMS LTD

Supplier Code of Conduct

Document codePRC-SCC-01
Effective date1 January 2025
OwnerHead of Procurement
ClassificationPublic

1. Introduction

Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd expects every supplier to meet the standards in this code. Accepting the code in writing is a condition of becoming a Calderwick supplier, and suppliers must apply equivalent standards to their own subcontractors.

2. People and labour

  • No forced, bonded or child labour.
  • Wages at least equal to the legal minimum, paid on time.
  • Working hours within legal limits.
  • Freedom of association and no discrimination.

3. Health and safety

Suppliers provide safe workplaces and follow Calderwick's site safety rules when working on our sites, including the use of personal protective equipment. Supplier staff working on Calderwick sites must hold the certificates their work requires.

4. Business integrity

Suppliers must not offer bribes or improper payments. Gifts or hospitality to Calderwick employees must not exceed USD 50 per occasion and must never be offered during a tender. Suppliers must disclose any family or financial link with a Calderwick employee involved in buying from them.

5. Environment

Suppliers comply with environmental law, reduce waste and emissions, and tell Calderwick about any environmental incident linked to work for Calderwick within 48 hours.

6. Information and data

Suppliers protect Calderwick information, use it only for the work ordered and follow the remote access rules of Calderwick's IT Security Policy when they connect to Calderwick systems.

7. Reporting concerns and audits

Suppliers and their staff can report concerns about Calderwick or its employees through the independent Clearline hotline. Calderwick may audit a supplier's compliance with this code with ten working days' notice. A serious breach of the code allows Calderwick to end the supplier's contracts.
