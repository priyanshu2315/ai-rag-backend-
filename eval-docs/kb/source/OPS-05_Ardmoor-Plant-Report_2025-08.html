1. Summary

Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd's Ardmoor plant completed 142 customer tests in August 2025 against a plan of 150. The shortfall came from the outage of rig TR-3 on 14 August (incident INC-2025-014) and the repeat of one customer endurance test on 16 August, which cost USD 41,000.

3. Incidents

  • 14 August: TR-3 out of service from 06:40 to 18:15 after a PLC controller failure (INC-2025-014). The controller from idle rig TR-1 was used to restore TR-3.
  • 14 August: a hydraulic hose on TR-2 burst during a pressure test. It was replaced the same day under the emergency purchase rule (purchase order PO-10229, approved by the Plant Manager).

4. Actions

  • Controller firmware updated on all rigs on 21 August.
  • PLC controllers added to the critical spares list on 25 August; one replacement and one spare ordered on PO-10234.
  • Customer test schedule for September re-planned to recover four of the eight lost tests.
