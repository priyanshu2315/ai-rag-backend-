1. Parties

This agreement is made between Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd ("Calderwick") and Brennock Valve Services Ltd ("the Supplier"), registered in Calderwick's vendor master as VEN-014.

2. Term

The agreement starts on 1 September 2024 and ends on 31 August 2026, a term of 24 months. It may be extended only by a written amendment signed by both parties.

3. Scope of services

The Supplier provides preventive and corrective maintenance for the four hydraulic test rigs TR-1, TR-2, TR-3 and TR-4 at Plant P2, Ardmoor. Preventive maintenance follows the schedule agreed each quarter with the Maintenance Lead, Ardmoor. Corrective maintenance covers any fault reported by the Calderwick service desk.

The services include labour, travel, diagnostic equipment and consumables used during a visit. They do not include replacement parts. Calderwick buys replacement parts on separate purchase orders, from the Supplier or from any other supplier.

4. Fees and invoicing

Calderwick pays a fixed monthly fee of USD 18,500, excluding tax, for all services in clause 3. The Supplier invoices monthly in arrears. Invoices are paid 45 days after receipt. Any service credits due under clause 7 are deducted from the next invoice.

6. Service levels

The Supplier must meet the response and restore times set out in Appendix A. Appendix A also defines the incident priorities, how the times are measured and the service credits that apply when a time is missed.

7. Service credits

When a service level in Appendix A is missed, Calderwick is entitled to the service credit stated there. The Calderwick contract owner claims the credit in writing within 30 days after the end of the month in which the incident occurred. A credit that is not claimed within that period is lost. Service credits are Calderwick's sole financial remedy for missed service levels, except as stated in clause 8.

8. Termination

Either party may end this agreement with 90 days' written notice. Calderwick may end it immediately if the Supplier misses the P1 restore time three or more times in any period of six months.

9. Signatures

Signed for Calderwick by the Head of Procurement and for the Supplier by its Operations Director on 22 August 2024.

Appendix A - Service Levels and Service Credits

Times are clock hours, counted from the moment the Calderwick service desk logs the call to the Supplier. Response means a qualified Supplier engineer is on site at the rig. Restore means the rig is back in service for testing.
