ClassificationInternal

1. Purpose

This document sets who may sign contracts on behalf of Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd. Signing a contract is different from approving a purchase order: purchase orders follow the Purchase Approval Authority Matrix, while this document decides who signs the contract itself.

2. Signing limits

Limits apply to the total value of the contract over its full term, excluding tax. For an amendment, use the total value of the contract after the amendment.

Table 1 - Signing limits

Total contract value (USD)Who may sign
Up to 50,000A procurement manager or the Head of Procurement
50,001 to 500,000Head of Procurement
500,001 to 2,000,000Chief Financial Officer
Above 2,000,000Chief Executive Officer, after a Board resolution

3. Additional signatures

  • Contracts with a term longer than 24 months are also signed by the General Counsel.
  • Where the Board authorises a named person to sign a contract, that person signs, whatever the value.
  • Employment contracts are signed by the Head of People.
  • Non-disclosure agreements may be signed by the General Counsel or the Contracts Counsel.

4. Contracts before 1 January 2025

Contracts signed before 1 January 2025 were signed under the previous rules and remain valid. Amendments to them made on or after 1 January 2025 follow this document.
