1. Summary

On 14 August 2025 test rig TR-3 at the Ardmoor plant of Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd stopped during a customer endurance test. The service desk logged the incident as P1 and called the maintenance supplier. The fault was a failed PLC controller on the rig's hydraulic power unit. No spare controller was held on site, so the rig stayed out of service until a controller was taken from rig TR-1, which was idle that day. TR-3 was back in service the same evening. The customer test was repeated on 16 August 2025.

3. Measured times

Measured from the service desk log at 06:55: the supplier engineer arrived after 1 hour 35 minutes, and the rig was back in service after 11 hours 20 minutes.

4. Root cause

The controller failed after a firmware fault corrupted its configuration memory. The same firmware version runs on the controllers of TR-1, TR-2 and TR-4. The firmware was updated on all four rigs on 21 August 2025 by the OT Systems Engineer. A contributing cause was that Ardmoor held no spare controller.
