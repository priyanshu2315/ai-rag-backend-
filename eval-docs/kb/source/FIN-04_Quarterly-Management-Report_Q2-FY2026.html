1. Summary

Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd recorded revenue of USD 25.1 million in the second quarter of fiscal year 2026, USD 0.2 million above budget, and EBITDA of USD 3.6 million, on budget. The delayed spare parts orders from the first quarter were delivered in July and August. For the first half year, revenue is USD 48.7 million against a budget of USD 48.9 million.

Test and calibration revenue at Ardmoor was reduced by two rig outages, on TR-3 in August (incident INC-2025-014) and on TR-1 in September (incident INC-2025-021). Lost test revenue is estimated at USD 0.12 million, and repeating one customer endurance test cost USD 41,000.

5. Capital projects

Capital spending of USD 1.6 million in the quarter included the deposit of USD 345,000 for the Fenwick Bay furnace relining (PRJ-14), approved by the Board on 18 September 2025, and the first engineering payments for the Ardmoor rig control upgrade (PRJ-11). Internal Audit found that the PRJ-11 engineering order was split into two purchase orders; see internal audit report IA-2025-04.

6. Outlook

The November furnace shutdown at Fenwick Bay will reduce castings output by about 1,300 tonnes in the third quarter; castings needed by Hollins Cross were built up in advance. Full-year revenue is still expected to reach the budget of USD 98.5 million.
