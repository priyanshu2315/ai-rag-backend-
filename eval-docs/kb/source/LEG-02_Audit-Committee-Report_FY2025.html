1. Members and meetings

The Audit Committee of Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd has three non-executive members: Rafael Okonjo (chair), Wen Sorensen and Helena Marsh. It met four times in FY2025. The CFO, the General Counsel and the external and internal auditors attend by invitation.

2. External audit

Pembury & Vale LLP audited the FY2025 accounts and issued an unqualified opinion on 22 May 2025. The audit fee was USD 186,000. The main audit matters were inventory valuation at Fenwick Bay and revenue recognition for multi-stage customer tests at Ardmoor. No material weaknesses were reported.

3. Internal audit

4. Whistleblowing

Five reports were received through the Clearline hotline and other channels in FY2025. Two were substantiated: an undeclared gift from a haulage supplier and a breach of forklift rules. Both led to action. No report was about senior management.

5. Risk management

The Committee reviewed the risk register each quarter. It asked management to pay particular attention to dependence on single suppliers for critical services and to the cyber security of plant control systems.
