3. Governance

Project managers report progress to their sponsor every month and to the executive team every quarter. A change that raises a project's budget by more than 5% needs the sponsor's approval; more than 10% needs the approval of whoever approved the original budget. Project PRJ-13 (a paint shop extension) was cancelled in March 2025 and is not listed.
