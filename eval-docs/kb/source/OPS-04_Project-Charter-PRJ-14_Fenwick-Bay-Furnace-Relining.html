1. Why this project

The refractory lining of the main induction furnace at Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd's Fenwick Bay foundry reached the end of its life. The condition survey by Kelvar Furnace Services Ltd in August 2025 (purchase order PO-10226) found lining wear of up to 60% in the melt zone. Relining is needed before the end of 2025 to avoid an unplanned failure.

2. Scope and supplier

Kelvar Furnace Services Ltd (vendor ID VEN-089) relines the furnace under contract CON-2025-019, signed on 22 September 2025. A deposit of USD 345,000 was paid on purchase order PO-10259; the balance is paid on completion and acceptance.

4. Shutdown plan

The foundry shuts from 3 to 24 November 2025. To keep Hollins Cross supplied, extra castings were bought from Hartwell Steel Castings Ltd in September on purchase order PO-10256. Castings staff are redeployed to support work and training during the shutdown; no annual leave is deducted.

6. Risks and safety

The main risks are R-09 (the relining overruns the shutdown) and R-10 (late renewal of the emissions permit). Work inside the furnace is confined-space work; it needs a permit issued by the Health and Safety Manager for each shift.
