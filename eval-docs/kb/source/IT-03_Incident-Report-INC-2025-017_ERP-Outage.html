1. Summary

On 26 August 2025 the Ledgerline ERP of Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd was unavailable to all users for just over six hours. Purchasing, goods receipts and invoice processing stopped at all sites. The cause was a database patch that the supplier, Quillon Software Ltd, applied the evening before without approval of the Change Advisory Board.

2. Timeline (26 August 2025)

Table 1 - Timeline

TimeEvent
08:05Service desk logged INC-2025-017 as P1 after users at all sites reported that Ledgerline would not open.
08:20Quillon Software Ltd support engaged under contract CON-2023-044.
09:40Cause found: a database patch applied by Quillon at 21:30 on 25 August had failed part-way.
11:15Decision to roll back the patch from the backup taken at 21:00 on 25 August.
14:10Ledgerline restored and checked by Finance; users informed.

3. Measured times and targets

Ledgerline was unavailable for 6 hours 5 minutes from the service desk log. The restore target in Quillon's support contract CON-2023-044 is 4 hours, so the supplier missed it. The recovery time objective for Ledgerline in the Disaster Recovery Plan is 8 hours; the outage stayed within it and disaster recovery was not invoked. No data was lost because no transactions had been entered after the 21:00 backup.

4. Impact

About 180 users could not work in Ledgerline during the outage. The August month-end close finished on working day 6 instead of working day 5.
