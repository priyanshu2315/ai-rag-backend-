4. Brennock Valve Services Ltd

Brennock missed the P1 restore time once in the six months to 30 September 2025 (INC-2025-014). Clause 8 of CON-2024-031 allows immediate termination only after three missed P1 restore times in any six months, so that right has not arisen. Incident INC-2025-021 was a P2 incident and does not count towards it.

Amendment CON-2024-031-A1, effective 1 October 2025, adds rig TR-5, raises the monthly fee to USD 19,800 and shortens the P1 restore time to 6 hours. Risk R-07 (single maintenance supplier for the test rigs) stays High until a second supplier is qualified.

5. Actions

  • Procurement Manager, Plant Services: start qualifying a second test rig maintenance supplier by 31 December 2025.
  • Head of IT: review the Quillon support contract before its renewal in March 2026.
  • Procurement Manager, Direct Materials: watch Tarnfield's on-time rate in October after the September dip.
