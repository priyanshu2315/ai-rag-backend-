2. Purchase approval authority matrix

The CFO presented a new Purchase Approval Authority Matrix for Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd, replacing the previous approval list, which had no co-approval rules. The matrix sets five approval bands by purchase order value and three co-approval rules for IT and OT purchases, long contracts and capital projects.

Resolution 2024-03: The Board approved the Purchase Approval Authority Matrix, Version 1 (FIN-AAM-01), with effect from 1 April 2024.

3. Budget FY2025

The CFO presented the budget for the year from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025.

Resolution 2024-04: The Board approved the FY2025 budget with revenue of USD 90.0 million, EBITDA of USD 12.6 million and capital expenditure of USD 5.8 million.

4. Ardmoor test capacity

The COO reported that customer demand for testing at Ardmoor exceeded capacity. The Board supported running rig TR-4 on two shifts from September 2024, funded within the FY2025 capital budget.
