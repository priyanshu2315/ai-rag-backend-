1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets the rules for business travel and for claiming business expenses at Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd. It applies to all employees and to contractors whose contract says that Calderwick pays their travel. Expenses are reimbursed only when they are necessary for Calderwick business, within the limits below and supported by receipts.

2. Booking travel

All flights, rail tickets and hotels are booked through the company travel desk, Corvia Travel, unless the travel desk cannot offer the trip. Book at least 14 days before travel where possible. Travel must be approved before booking, using the approval limits in section 6.

4. Hotels

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to the nightly caps below, including breakfast and excluding tax. Major cities are London, Frankfurt, Singapore, New York and Dubai; all other locations are standard locations.

5. Meals and daily allowance

Instead of claiming individual meals, travellers receive a daily allowance: USD 55 for each full day away from home and USD 25 for a day trip of more than 8 hours. No meal receipts are needed for the allowance. Meals with customers are claimed separately as business entertainment with receipts and the names of the guests.

7. Making a claim

Submit expense claims in the finance system within 30 days of returning from the trip. Attach receipts for every item above USD 25. Claims submitted after the deadline are paid only with the approval of the Financial Controller. Approved claims are paid with the next weekly payment run.

8. Not reimbursed

  • Upgrades to a higher travel class than allowed
  • Minibar, films and personal phone calls
  • Fines for traffic or parking offences
  • Lost personal property
  • Expenses of family members or guests travelling with you
