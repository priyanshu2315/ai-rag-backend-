1. Background

Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd ("Calderwick") and Brennock Valve Services Ltd ("the Supplier") are parties to Maintenance Services Agreement CON-2024-031 for the hydraulic test rigs at Ardmoor. In the quarter from July to September 2025 the Supplier missed service levels on two incidents, INC-2025-014 and INC-2025-021. On 18 September 2025 the Board of Calderwick asked the Head of Procurement to issue a formal warning and to agree shorter restore times (Resolution 2025-11). A fifth test rig, TR-5, entered service at Ardmoor on 29 September 2025.

2. Changes

From 1 October 2025 the agreement is amended as follows:

Table 1 - Changes

ClauseBefore this amendmentFrom 1 October 2025
Clause 3, scopeTest rigs TR-1 to TR-4Test rigs TR-1 to TR-5
Clause 4, monthly feeUSD 18,500USD 19,800
Appendix A, P1 restore time8 hours6 hours
New clause 5A, on-call engineerNoneA Supplier engineer is on call for Ardmoor from 07:00 to 22:00 every day and on site within 2 hours of a P1 call

All other terms of CON-2024-031 stay unchanged, including the P1 response time of 2 hours, the P2 and P3 service levels, the service credit percentages, the 15% monthly cap and clause 8 on termination.

3. Formal warning

Calderwick records that the Supplier missed the P1 restore time on incident INC-2025-014 and the P2 response and restore times on incident INC-2025-021. This amendment serves as the formal warning requested by the Board. Calderwick's right to end the agreement immediately under clause 8 remains.

4. Incidents before 1 October 2025

Incidents logged before 1 October 2025 are measured against the service levels and fee in force when they were logged.

5. Signatures

Signed on 26 September 2025 for Calderwick by Linnea Haugland, Head of Procurement, and for the Supplier by Rhiannon Pryce, Operations Director.
