1. Background and scope

Fairholm Assurance LLP is the internal auditor of Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd. This audit checked whether purchase orders approved in the second quarter of fiscal year 2026 were approved by the right people. It was the first audit after the amended Purchase Approval Authority Matrix (FIN-AAM-02) took effect on 1 July 2025.

We tested all 20 purchase orders in the Purchase Order Log for July to September 2025 against the matrix version that applied on the date each order was raised, the co-approval rules, the emergency purchase rule and the rule against splitting orders. Acting approvals were checked against the staff directory (HR-DIR-2025-07).

2. Overall conclusion

Approvals were mostly well controlled: 19 of 20 orders were approved correctly. We found one high-rated issue, a split order, and two lower-rated matters.

4. Areas found compliant

  • All orders raised from 1 July 2025 were approved under FIN-AAM-02, and PO-10189, raised on 27 June 2025, correctly under FIN-AAM-01.
  • Orders approved by Marcus Ebner between 7 and 20 August 2025 fall within his acting appointment as Head of Procurement (4 to 22 August 2025).
  • Co-approval by the Head of IT was obtained for every IT and OT order above USD 20,000 (PO-10207, PO-10211 and PO-10234).
