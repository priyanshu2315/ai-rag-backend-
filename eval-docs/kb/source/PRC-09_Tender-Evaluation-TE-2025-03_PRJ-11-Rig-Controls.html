1. Purpose

Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd invited bids for the engineering and integration of new control systems for the five hydraulic test rigs at Ardmoor under project PRJ-11. Because the estimated value was above USD 150,000, the Procurement Policy required a formal evaluation even though Ardent Controls Ltd holds a framework agreement (CON-2025-008).

3. Method

Bids were scored 60% on technical merit and 40% on price. The price score is the lowest bid price divided by the bidder's price, multiplied by 40.

Table 2 - Scores

Ardent Controls Ltd (VEN-052)192,50052.032.884.8

Ardent scored highest on technical merit because its design uses the same AC-HPU-7 controller family already installed on the rigs, needs only one week of rig downtime per rig and includes the firmware already in use at Ardmoor.

5. Recommendation and approval

The panel recommends awarding the work to Ardent Controls Ltd for USD 192,500, ordered under framework agreement CON-2025-008. Under the approval matrix in force (FIN-AAM-02), an order of this value needs the Chief Financial Officer (band C) and, as a capital project above USD 100,000, the Chief Operating Officer (rule CA-3).

The award was approved by Tobias Renwick, Chief Financial Officer, and Adaeze Okafor, Chief Operating Officer, on 5 August 2025.
