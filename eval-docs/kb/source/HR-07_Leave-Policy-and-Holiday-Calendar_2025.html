1. Annual leave

Full-time employees of Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd receive 25 days of paid annual leave in each leave year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount. The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

After every five complete years of service, employees receive one extra day of annual leave per year, up to a maximum of 30 days.

2. Booking and carrying over leave

Book leave in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences of up to five days, and at least four weeks in advance for longer absences. Your line manager may refuse a request when too many people in the team are already away.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be taken by 30 June; any carried-over days not taken by then are lost. More days may be carried over only when Calderwick cancelled approved leave for business reasons.
