1. Parties and purpose

This framework agreement is between Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd ("Calderwick") and Ardent Controls Ltd ("the Supplier"), vendor ID VEN-052. It sets prices and terms for controls hardware, firmware and engineering services for the hydraulic test rigs at Plant P2, Ardmoor. It does not oblige Calderwick to buy any minimum quantity.

2. Term and approvals

The agreement runs for 36 months, from 1 February 2025 to 31 January 2028. Because its term is longer than 24 months, it was co-approved by the General Counsel on 20 January 2025 under rule CA-2 of the approval matrix. It was signed for Calderwick by the Head of Procurement and the General Counsel, and for the Supplier by its Managing Director, Corin Ashby.

3. Ordering

Calderwick orders under this agreement by sending a purchase order (a call-off) that quotes CON-2025-008. Each purchase order is approved under Calderwick's approval matrix. Under Calderwick's Procurement Policy, a single call-off above USD 150,000 needs a written evaluation of the Supplier's offer before it is placed.

4. Prices

The prices in Schedule 1 are fixed until 31 January 2026. On each 1 February after that, the Supplier may raise them by the change in the consumer price index over the previous year, up to a maximum of 3%.

5. Delivery and warranty

Stocked items in Schedule 1 are delivered to Ardmoor within 15 business days of the purchase order. Engineering services start on the date agreed in the purchase order. Hardware is guaranteed for 24 months from delivery; the Supplier replaces faulty hardware free of charge within 5 business days of notice.
