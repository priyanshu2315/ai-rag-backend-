1. Why this project

The hydraulic test rigs at Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd's Ardmoor plant run on control software and panels installed between 2012 and 2016. Spare parts are scarce, the firmware has known faults, and the old controls cannot be separated from the office network, which is a cyber security risk. The project replaces the control systems of all five rigs with a common design.

2. Scope

  • New control engineering and integration for rigs TR-1, TR-2, TR-3, TR-4 and TR-5.
  • New AC-HPU-7 PLC controllers on the hydraulic power units of all five rigs, the same controller family already used at Ardmoor; the controllers removed are kept as spares.
  • New operator panels (HMI) on all five rigs.
  • A separate, segmented OT network for the rigs with vendor access only through the jump host.
  • Out of scope: mechanical work on the rigs and the test hall building.

Rig TR-5 was transferred from Hollins Cross, mechanically refurbished and entered service at Ardmoor on 29 September 2025 with its old controls; it receives the new controls in March 2026.
