1. Summary

On 9 September 2025 rig TR-1 at the Ardmoor plant of Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd developed a hydraulic leak at a cylinder manifold. The rig kept running at reduced pressure for low-pressure tests only, so the service desk logged the incident as P2 (rig working with reduced capacity). The maintenance supplier, Brennock Valve Services Ltd, arrived late and the repair took more than two days because a cracked fitting had to be replaced.

4. Root cause

A fitting on the cylinder manifold cracked from fatigue. The same fitting type is used on rigs TR-2 to TR-4; they were inspected during the next preventive maintenance visits and no cracks were found.
