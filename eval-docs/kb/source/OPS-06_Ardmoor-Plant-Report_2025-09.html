1. Summary

Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd's Ardmoor plant completed 151 customer tests in September 2025 against a plan of 155. Rig TR-1 was degraded for two days after a hydraulic leak on 9 September (incident INC-2025-021). Rig TR-5, transferred from Hollins Cross, entered service on 29 September.

3. Incidents

  • 9 to 11 September: TR-1 ran with reduced pressure after a hydraulic leak (INC-2025-021). The maintenance supplier arrived 6 hours 30 minutes after the call and the rig was fully restored on 11 September at 10:00.
  • 17 September: a technician cut a hand on a sharp bracket; first aid only.

4. Supplier and projects

After two missed service levels in two months, the maintenance contract with Brennock Valve Services Ltd was amended from 1 October 2025: TR-5 is added, the P1 restore time is shortened to 6 hours and an on-call engineer covers Ardmoor from 07:00 to 22:00. The PRJ-11 design review with Ardent Controls Ltd took place on 24 September; design freeze was confirmed for 30 September.
