1. Summary

Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd recorded two lost-time injuries in fiscal year 2025, the same number as in FY2024. The lost-time injury frequency rate fell from 1.7 to 1.6 per million hours worked because hours rose. Near-miss reporting rose from 33 to 41 reports, which we welcome as a sign of better reporting.

5. Training and certificates

96% of forklift operators held a valid certificate on 31 March 2025; two operators were stood down until renewal. All test rig operators at Ardmoor held a valid test rig certificate. Fire warden coverage met the minimum of two per shift at every site.

6. Priorities for FY2026

  • Physical separation of forklift and pedestrian routes at Hollins Cross.
  • Hearing protection compliance in the Ardmoor test halls while rigs are running.
  • Confined-space planning for the Fenwick Bay furnace relining in November 2025.
  • Monthly certificate checks against the training register at every plant.
