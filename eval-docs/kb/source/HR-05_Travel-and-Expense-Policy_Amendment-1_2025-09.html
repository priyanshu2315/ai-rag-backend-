1. What this amendment changes

This amendment changes four rules of the Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd Travel and Expense Policy (HR-TEP-01): the hotel caps in Table 2, the daily allowance in section 5, the flight class threshold in Table 1 and the claim deadline in section 7. All other rules of HR-TEP-01 stay in force, including the approval limits in Table 3 and the mileage rate.

The new rules apply to trips that start on or after 1 September 2025. Trips that started before that date follow HR-TEP-01 as originally issued.

2. Reason

Hotel prices in major cities rose faster than the caps set in April 2024, and many travellers were paying the difference themselves. Long-haul flights to customers in Asia were also exceeding 6 hours by small margins, which made premium economy the default for routes where economy is acceptable.
