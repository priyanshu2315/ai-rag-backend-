1. Highlights

Fiscal year 2025 was a record year for Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd. Revenue grew 7% to USD 92.4 million, ahead of the budget of USD 90.0 million, driven by water utility orders and strong demand for test and calibration services at Ardmoor. EBITDA rose to USD 13.1 million (budget USD 12.6 million) and net profit to USD 6.8 million.

All amounts in this summary are in USD million unless stated otherwise. Fiscal year 2025 (FY2025) runs from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025; the comparative year FY2024 ran from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2024.

4. Costs

Cost of sales rose 5.9%, less than revenue, so the gross margin improved from 29.8% to 30.5%. Steel castings prices rose about 3% during the year; the effect was offset by higher volumes at Fenwick Bay, which poured 8,950 tonnes of castings, and by productivity gains at Hollins Cross, which assembled 14,380 pumps.

Operating expenses rose 7.9% to USD 15.1 million, mainly from 16 additional employees in engineering and test services and from the new internal audit contract with Fairholm Assurance LLP, which started on 1 January 2025.

8. Outlook for FY2026

The Board approved the FY2026 budget on 13 March 2025 (Resolution 2025-03): revenue of USD 98.5 million, EBITDA of USD 14.2 million and capital expenditure of USD 7.4 million, including the Ardmoor rig control upgrade, the Hollins Cross assembly line 3 automation and the Fenwick Bay furnace relining. The main risks are steel prices, the availability of test rigs at Ardmoor and the timing of the furnace relining.
