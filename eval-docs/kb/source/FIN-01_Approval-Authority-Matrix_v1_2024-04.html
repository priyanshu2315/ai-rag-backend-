1. Purpose and scope

This matrix sets who must approve a purchase order (PO) before it is sent to a supplier. It applies to every PO raised by any Calderwick site, including the Head Office and Plants P1, P2 and P3. It does not cover payroll, tax payments or intercompany charges.

2. How to use this matrix

The PO value is the total value of the order in USD, excluding tax. Find the band for that value in Table 1; the approver for that band must approve the PO. Then check Table 2: if a co-approval rule applies, the co-approver must also approve. A PO needs every approval that applies before it is released.

Approvers are named by role. The person holding a role, or acting in it, is listed in the current staff directory published by the Head of People.

5. Emergency purchases

When equipment failure stops production or testing, the Plant Manager of the affected plant may approve an emergency repair purchase up to USD 15,000 without waiting for the band approver. The band approver must ratify the PO within 5 working days. Emergency approval cannot be used for planned replacements or for spares bought after the repair is complete.

6. Splitting orders

A purchase must not be split into several POs to bring each PO into a lower band. Internal Audit reviews POs to the same supplier raised within 30 days of each other, and any split found is reported to the Chief Financial Officer.

7. Review

The Chief Financial Officer reviews this matrix every year. Any change must be approved by the Board of Directors and is issued as a numbered amendment that states which parts of this version it replaces.
