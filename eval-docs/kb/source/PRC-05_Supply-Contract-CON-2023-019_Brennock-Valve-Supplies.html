1. Parties

This contract is between Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd ("Calderwick") and Brennock Valve Supplies Ltd ("the Supplier"), vendor ID VEN-041. The Supplier is not related to Brennock Valve Services Ltd, which maintains Calderwick's test rigs under a separate contract.

2. Scope

The Supplier supplies cast-iron gate valves to Plant P1, Hollins Cross, for fitting to industrial pumps during assembly. Calderwick orders by purchase order quoting CON-2023-019.

4. Delivery and late delivery

The Supplier delivers within 20 business days of the purchase order. For each business day of late delivery, Calderwick may deduct 0.5% of the value of the late items, up to a maximum of 10% of that value.

5. Quality

Each valve is pressure tested by the Supplier before dispatch and delivered with its test certificate. Valves that fail Calderwick's incoming inspection are returned and replaced within 10 business days at the Supplier's cost.

6. Contract management

Calderwick's contract owner is the Procurement Manager, Direct Materials. Quality questions go to the Quality Manager. The contract owners meet once a quarter.

7. Ending the contract

Either party may end the contract with 60 days' written notice. The contract ends automatically on 28 February 2026 unless both parties sign a renewal.
