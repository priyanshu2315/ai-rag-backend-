The Chair confirmed a quorum. No director declared a conflict of interest. The meeting was held at Ardmoor and directors toured the test halls.

2. Trading update

The CFO reported that revenue for April and May 2025 was slightly below budget because of delayed spare parts orders, which were expected in July.

3. Amendment of the purchase approval matrix

The CFO explained that many routine orders between USD 50,001 and USD 75,000 were reaching the CFO under FIN-AAM-01, and proposed higher limits for the Head of Procurement, lower thresholds for CEO and Board approval, and a lower IT and OT co-approval threshold.

Resolution 2025-07: The Board approved Amendment No. 1 to the Purchase Approval Authority Matrix (FIN-AAM-02), replacing Table 1 and rule CA-1 of FIN-AAM-01, with effect from 1 July 2025. All other parts of FIN-AAM-01 remain in force.

4. Ardmoor rig control upgrade

The COO presented project PRJ-11 to replace the control systems of the Ardmoor test rigs and add a fifth rig, TR-5, transferred from Hollins Cross. The project addresses the age of the controls and the cyber risk of the current network design.

Resolution 2025-08: The Board approved project PRJ-11 with a budget of USD 480,000 and appointed the COO as sponsor.

5. Next meeting

The next meeting will be held on 18 September 2025.
