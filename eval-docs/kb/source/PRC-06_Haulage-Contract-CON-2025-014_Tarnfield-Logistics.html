1. Parties and scope

This contract is between Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd ("Calderwick") and Tarnfield Logistics Ltd ("the Supplier"), vendor ID VEN-061. The Supplier carries finished pumps from Hollins Cross to customers and runs the castings shuttle from Fenwick Bay to Hollins Cross.

2. Term

The contract runs for 24 months, from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2027.

3. Rates

Table 1 - Rates

Hollins Cross to customer, zone A (up to 250 km)640
Hollins Cross to customer, zone B (251 to 600 km)910

Waiting time beyond 2 hours75 per hour

The Supplier invoices monthly for the loads carried in the month.

4. Performance

Performance is measured each month as the share of loads delivered within the agreed delivery window. The target is 95%. For each full percentage point below 95% in a month, Calderwick receives a credit of 3% of that month's charges. For example, 93.4% gives one full point below target and a credit of 3%. Credits are claimed by the contract owner within 30 days after the month end.

5. Contract management

Calderwick's contract owner is the Procurement Manager, Direct Materials. The Supplier provides a monthly performance report by the fifth working day of the next month.

6. Insurance and liability

The Supplier insures goods in transit up to USD 250,000 per load. Pumps with a higher value need a special insurance arrangement agreed before dispatch.
