1. Summary

Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd's Hollins Cross plant assembled 1,240 pumps in August 2025, 40 above plan. First-pass quality was 98.2%. Work on assembly line 3 automation (PRJ-12) reached 55% completion.

3. Materials

Gate valves from Brennock Valve Supplies Ltd ordered on PO-10203 arrived on 30 July, ending the valve shortage of June and July. Castings supply from Fenwick Bay was normal. Extra castings will be bought from Hartwell Steel Castings Ltd in September to cover the Fenwick Bay furnace shutdown in November.

4. Safety

On 12 August a forklift reversed close to a pedestrian in aisle 4; no one was hurt. The Health and Safety Manager has ordered physical barriers between forklift and walking routes in aisles 3 to 6, to be installed in October. Supervisors were reminded to check forklift certificates in the training register before each shift.

5. Projects

PRJ-12: the robot cell for line 3 was installed in August; commissioning is planned for October 2025 and handover by 31 January 2026. The solar canopy over the car park (PRJ-16) was ordered in July and will be installed in October.
