The Chair confirmed a quorum. No director declared a conflict of interest. The minutes of 19 June 2025 were approved.

2. Trading update

The CFO reported that first-quarter revenue of USD 23.6 million was USD 0.4 million below budget and that July and August had recovered most of the gap.

3. Test rig maintenance supplier

The Head of Procurement reported that Brennock Valve Services Ltd, the maintenance supplier for the Ardmoor test rigs under contract CON-2024-031, had missed service levels on two incidents: INC-2025-014 on rig TR-3 in August (P1 restore time) and INC-2025-021 on rig TR-1 in September (P2 response and restore times). Service credits had been or would be claimed. No second qualified supplier is available at short notice.

Resolution 2025-11: The Board asked the Head of Procurement to issue a formal warning to Brennock Valve Services Ltd, to agree a P1 restore time of no more than 6 hours with effect from 1 October 2025, and to qualify a second maintenance supplier by 31 December 2025.

4. Head of IT

The CEO reported that Dev Malhotra, Head of IT, has resigned and that his last working day is 31 October 2025. Recruitment of a permanent successor has started.

Resolution 2025-12: The Board appointed Yusuf Karimi, Information Security Officer, as interim Head of IT from 1 November 2025 until a permanent Head of IT starts, with the full authority of the Head of IT, including co-approval under rule CA-1 of the purchase approval matrix.

5. Fenwick Bay furnace relining

The COO presented project PRJ-14 to reline the main induction furnace at Fenwick Bay during a shutdown from 3 to 24 November 2025, following the condition survey by Kelvar Furnace Services Ltd. Because the budget is above USD 750,000, Board approval is needed under the approval matrix.

Resolution 2025-13: The Board approved project PRJ-14 with a budget of USD 1,150,000, approved the award of the relining works to Kelvar Furnace Services Ltd under contract CON-2025-019, and authorised the CFO to sign the contract.

6. Next meeting

The next meeting will be held on 11 December 2025.
