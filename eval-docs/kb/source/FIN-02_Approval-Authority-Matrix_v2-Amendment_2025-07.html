1. What this amendment changes

This amendment to the purchase approval matrix of Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd replaces Table 1 (approval bands) and rule CA-1 of FIN-AAM-01. Every other part of FIN-AAM-01 stays in force without change, including rules CA-2 and CA-3, the emergency purchase rule in section 5 and the rule against splitting orders in section 6.

The band that applies to a purchase order is set by the date the PO is raised. A PO raised on or after 1 July 2025 follows this amendment. A PO raised before 1 July 2025 follows FIN-AAM-01, even if it is approved after that date.

2. Reason for the change

Since April 2024 the number of POs between USD 50,001 and USD 75,000 reaching the Chief Financial Officer grew sharply, mostly routine spare parts and maintenance work for the plants. The Board moved those orders to the Head of Procurement and lowered the CEO and Board thresholds so that larger commitments receive more senior review. The IT and OT co-approval threshold was lowered because controllers and plant software now make up a larger share of spending.

4. New rule CA-1

Rule CA-1 now reads: IT and OT (operational technology) hardware or software with a PO value above USD 20,000 must also be approved by the Head of IT. The threshold in FIN-AAM-01 was USD 25,000.
