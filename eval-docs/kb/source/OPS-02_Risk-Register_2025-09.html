1. How to read this register

Each risk of Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd has a unique ID and an owner, named by role. The owner keeps the entry up to date and runs the mitigation. Ratings combine likelihood and impact: High, Medium or Low. The person holding each role is listed in the current staff directory.
