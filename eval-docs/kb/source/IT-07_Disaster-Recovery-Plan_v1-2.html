1. Purpose

This plan explains how Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd restores its IT and plant control systems after a major failure, such as the loss of a server room, a cyber attack or a long outage of a key system. It sets recovery targets, roles and the steps to invoke and run disaster recovery.

3. Recovery targets

The recovery time objective (RTO) is the longest time a system may be unavailable. The recovery point objective (RPO) is the most data, measured in time, that may be lost.

4. When to invoke

Disaster recovery is invoked when a system in Table 2 is unavailable, or is expected to be unavailable, for longer than its RTO, or immediately after a confirmed destructive cyber attack. Outages that are expected to end within the RTO are handled as normal incidents by the service desk.

5. Recovery steps

  • The DR coordinator confirms the scope of the failure and informs the invocation authority.
  • The invocation authority decides whether to invoke and records the time.
  • The DR coordinator opens a recovery bridge call and assigns recovery teams by system.
  • Systems are restored in the order of Table 2, with Ledgerline and email first.
  • Each restored system is checked by its business owner before users are told it is available.
  • The DR coordinator writes a recovery report within 10 working days.
