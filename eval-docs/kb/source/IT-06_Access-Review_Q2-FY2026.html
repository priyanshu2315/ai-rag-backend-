1. Scope

This review checked all user, privileged and supplier accounts on Calderwick Pump Systems Ltd systems as required by section 4 of the IT Security Policy (IT-SEC-02). Line managers confirmed the access of their teams; the Information Security Officer checked leavers against the HR portal and supplier accounts against the jump host records.
